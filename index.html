The Problem

Most people on the internet rely on a single password to secure all their accounts. This makes them vulnerable to serious cyberattacks. People are also never taught how to properly manage their passwords, leaving them exposed without even knowing it.

The risk is not just about passwords. From phishing emails to unsecured Wi-Fi, everyday online habits create openings that attackers actively exploit.

Mission

This site advocates for better password practices and cybersecurity awareness, especially for students. Security knowledge should be easy to access and understand, having a technical background is not required.

This is meant for students, parents, and anyone who wants to stay safe on the internet.
