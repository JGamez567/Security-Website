Teaching and Learning Cybersecurity Awareness with Gamification in Smaller Universities and Colleges
Matovu, R., Nwokeji, J. C., Holmes, T., & Rahman, T.

Examines the use of gamification to teach cybersecurity awareness to students, finding that over 97% of students felt motivated to learn cybersecurity concepts through gamified activities.
